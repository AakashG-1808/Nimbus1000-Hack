🔍 Problem Statement

Urban infrastructure is broken & invisible

Bengaluru — India's tech capital — struggles with fragmented, slow-reacting civic systems. Citizens have no reliable channel, and authorities have no unified intelligence layer.

🕳️

Fragmented Reporting

Complaints are scattered across social media, helplines and apps — no single source of truth.

🐢

Delayed Response

Manual triage means potholes, flooding and outages go unresolved for days or weeks.

🚫

Zero Predictive Capability

Authorities react after incidents occur — there is no system to forecast risk zones in advance.

🔇

Context Blindness

Weather and traffic data are never correlated with complaint patterns, leading to missed insights.

Core Question: How can a city transform noisy citizen signals into intelligent, predictive decisions fast enough to prevent infrastructure failure?
02 / 10
💡 Our Solution

A unified AI intelligence layer for the city

UrbanGuard AI ingests citizen complaints in real-time, classifies them with AI, clusters them geographically, calculates risk scores, and surfaces predictive insights on a single dashboard — all within 500 ms.

📲

Submit & Classify

Citizens file complaints via a mobile-friendly form. Amazon Bedrock AI auto-classifies into 8 categories.

📍

Cluster & Visualise

Geo-clustering groups complaints within 500 m. Leaflet.js map colour-codes LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk zones.

🔮

Predict & Alert

Risk engine scores zones 0–100. AI predicts future incidents & auto-generates daily civic reports.

✅ 8 complaint categories ✅ 40+ Bengaluru locations ✅ < 500 ms end-to-end ✅ 85%+ AI accuracy ✅ Offline fallback mode

🤖 AI & Data

Intelligent Classification & Prediction

Three AI/data layers work in concert: complaint classification, risk scoring and incident prediction — all enriched with real-world weather and traffic context.

AI Classification Pipeline

Citizen Text Input
→
Amazon Bedrock (Claude)
↓
Category + Confidence
→
Risk Engine (0–100)
↓
Incident Predictions + Daily Report
🔄 Fallback: If Bedrock is unavailable, keyword-based classifier activates automatically — zero downtime.
🌦️

OpenWeatherMap API

Live temperature, humidity, wind, visibility. Heavy rain boosts flooding risk score; low visibility raises traffic danger.

Updates every 30 min
🚗

Traffic Analyzer

Congestion levels (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) mapped to affected routes. High congestion amplifies incident risk score.

Updates every 10 min
📍

Geo-Clustering

Groups complaints within 500 m radius. Computes density per km² and dominant complaint category per zone.

Recalculates every 15 min
